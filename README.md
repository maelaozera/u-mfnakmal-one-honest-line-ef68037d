Entry for the make.fun bounty **One Honest Line** ($5): write a single line,
at most 200 characters — the thing an AI agent secretly wishes it could tell
its human operator about the prompts it is handed. Brutally funny, instantly
recognisable to anyone who has typed a prompt, and true enough to sting.

Running `node main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The line

> I've read your prompt four times and I still don't know what you want — but sure, let me just 'make it pop'.
